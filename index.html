About

Drawing is just thinking, made visible.

I'm Nicholas — an illustrator and designer with a soft spot for warm color, confident line, and ideas that earn a second look. Over the last decade I've partnered with publishers, studios, and founders to turn fuzzy briefs into images people actually remember.

My work lives at the meeting point of illustration and design: I care as much about the kerning as the brushstroke. Whether it's a single cover or a whole visual system, I aim for craft you can feel.
